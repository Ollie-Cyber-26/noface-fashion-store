03. The Tailor's Alteration Rules

Fit separates quiet luxury from standard off-the-rack clothing. Hand these exact instructions to your tailor:

1. Chino Shorts Hem Length (The 7-Inch Rule)

The hem must sit precisely 1.5 to 2 inches above the top of your kneecap when standing straight. Never allow shorts to touch or cover the knee. The leg opening should taper slightly to mirror your thigh width with 1 inch of slack.

2. Linen Trouser Break (No-Break or Slight Kiss)

Cream linen trousers look best with zero break: the hem should lightly touch the top of your shoe or slide without folding over. For linen's natural drape, request a 1.5-inch blind hem cuff for extra weight at the bottom.

3. Unstructured Blazer Sleeve Length

Blazer cuffs should end exactly at the wrist bone, exposing 1/4 to 1/2 inch of your shirt cuff. The jacket body must cover approximately 80% of your seat (not cropped, not overcoat length).

4. Pleated Trousers Rise & Drape

Pleated charcoal trousers must sit at your natural waist (just below the navel), not on the hips. This ensures the single pleat stays flat while standing and opens cleanly when seated.

04. Fabric Care & Longevity

Proper laundering ensures these 16 pieces look better over time rather than wearing out:

Linen Care
Washing & Ironing French Flax

Always wash linen in cold water on a delicate cycle. Never use high heat in a dryer (hang dry only). To achieve the signature casual drape, steam while slightly damp rather than pressing flat with a heavy iron.

Leather Care
Slides & Tote Bag Conditioning

Apply a neutral beeswax or lanolin leather balm once every 3 months. Wipe with a dry microfiber cloth after rain exposure. Store totes stuffed with tissue to maintain structural silhouette.

Footwear Maintenance
Cork Footbed Sealing

Apply cork sealant to the exposed edges of your sandals once a year to prevent water penetration and cracking. Clean white sneaker leather with gentle foam and a soft horsehair brush.

Tailoring Maintenance
Blazer Care (Dry Clean Sparingly)

Do not dry clean your unstructured blazer more than twice a year. Frequent chemical cleaning degrades wool fibers. Use a garment steamer between wears to release wrinkles and eliminate odors.
