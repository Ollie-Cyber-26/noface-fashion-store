Chapter 04 · Proportion & Longevity

The Tailor's Alteration & Care Rules

Exact hem, sleeve, and rise specifications to make $35 Amazon garments drape like $400 boutique tailoring.

Tailoring Rule 01

Chino Shorts Hem Length (The 7-Inch Rule)

The hem must sit precisely 1.5 to 2 inches above the top of your kneecap when standing straight. Never allow shorts to cover the knee. The leg opening should taper slightly to mirror your thigh width with 1 inch of slack.

Tailoring Rule 02

Linen Trouser Break (Zero Break or Slight Kiss)

Cream and navy linen trousers look sharpest with zero break: the hem should lightly kiss the top of your loafer or slide without bunching. Request a 1.5-inch blind hem cuff for extra weight and vertical drape at the ankle.

Tailoring Rule 03

Unstructured Blazer Sleeve Exposure

Blazer cuffs should end right at the wrist bone, exposing 1/4 to 1/2 inch of your linen or oxford shirt cuff. Unlined knit blazers should be steamed on a wide wooden hanger rather than pressed flat with an iron.

Tailoring Rule 04

Linen, Suede & Cork Care Protocol

Wash linen garments in cold water on a delicate cycle and hang dry (never tumble dry on high heat). Brush suede footwear with a dry crepe brush and condition full-grain leather bags and belts every 90 days with neutral beeswax balm.
